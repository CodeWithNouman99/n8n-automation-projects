==================================================
1. RESTAURANT OVERVIEW
==================================================
Name: Lahori Tikka House
Type: Family desi and BBQ restaurant
City: Faisalabad, Pakistan
Address: Susan Road, near Madina Town, Faisalabad
Speciality: Charcoal BBQ, karahi, handi and fresh tandoori naan
Food: 100% halal
Ambience: Family-friendly, traditional Lahori style

5. RESERVATION POLICY
==================================================
- Reservations can be made by phone call
- Details needed: name, date, time, number of guests, phone number
- Maximum 20 guests per normal booking
- More than 20 guests or full events: take name and number, manager will call back
- Table is held for 15 minutes after booking time, then released
- Rooftop booking depends on weather; the restaurant may shift guests to the Family Hall if it rains
- Friday night, Saturday and Sunday are busy, so booking 1 day in advance is recommended
- Booking for same day is allowed if tables are available
- No advance payment is needed for normal bookings
- Private Room booking needs manager confirmation

Cancellation & Changes:
- Bookings can be changed or cancelled by calling, using the same name and phone number
- Please cancel at least 2 hours before the booking time
- No cancellation fee

8. SPICE LEVEL & DIETARY INFO
==================================================
- All dishes can be made Mild, Medium or Spicy on request
- Mild options for kids: Chicken White Karahi, Malai Boti, Reshmi Kabab
- Vegetarian options: Daal Mash, Mix Sabzi, naan, raita, salad
- Most karahi dishes contain dairy (ghee, yogurt or cream)
- Customers with allergies should tell the staff; the manager can confirm ingredients

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
==================================================
Q: Do you have a family hall?
A: Yes, an air-conditioned Family Hall for up to 80 guests.

Q: Is the food halal?
A: Yes, all food is 100% halal.

Q: Can I book the rooftop?
A: Yes, depending on weather. If it rains, guests are moved to the Family Hall.

Q: What is your best dish?
A: Mutton Karahi and Malai Boti are the most popular. For groups, the Family Deal is the best value.

Q: Do you have something not spicy for kids?
A: Yes, Chicken White Karahi, Malai Boti and Reshmi Kabab are mild.

Q: How long will you hold my table?
A: 15 minutes after the booking time.

Q: Is there a charge to cancel?
A: No, but please cancel at least 2 hours before.

Q: Do you deliver?
A: Yes, within 5 km for 150 rupees delivery charge.

Q: Can I bring my own cake?
A: Yes, no extra charge.

Q: Do you have WiFi?
A: Yes, free WiFi for dine-in guests. Password is given by the waiter.

Q: Is there a prayer area?
A: Yes, a small prayer area is available on the ground floor.
